Curiosity

NASA wanted to know if Mars was ever habitable for life. Spirit and Opportunity found water, but Curiosity was a full mobile laboratory, it drills rocks and analyzes them inside it, with a 2.1 meter arm.

The journey was 560 million km, and 8 months in space. Landing was the hardest part, NASA called it 7 Minutes of Terror.

It entered the atmosphere at 21,000 km per hour, then parachute, then rockets, then a rocket crane called Sky Crane lowered it with cables to the ground, then the crane flew away.

In 2013 it found an ancient freshwater lake in Yellowknife Bay, with all elements needed for life, carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, sulfur, and phosphorus.

In 2015 NASA confirmed lakes and streams in Gale 3.3 to 3.8 billion years ago. In 2018 it found organic molecules in mudstone. In 2019 it found clay minerals, a sign of water.

In 2024 it found 21 organic molecules in one sample, the largest set found on Mars, with long-chain alkanes. It has traveled more than 32 km and climbed Mount Sharp.

Mount Sharp is 5 km high inside Gale Crater, Curiosity climbed 1 km in elevation, the highest ever climbed on Mars. Its wheels were damaged by sharp rocks, but NASA changed driving to protect them.

In August 2026 it reached 5,000 sols, and NASA celebrated with a panorama.

Curiosity Discovery

This image shows Curiosity's discovery in 2024 on Mars. The rover on the right is Curiosity in Gale Crater. On the left are 3 molecules it found: Decane C10H22, Undecane C11H24, Dodecane C12H26. Black balls are Carbon, white balls are Hydrogen. These are long-chain hydrocarbons, building blocks of life. Found inside mudstone in Gale Crater. This proves Mars had a lake that could have supported life billions of years ago.

Curiosity Holes

This image shows 42 drill holes made by Curiosity on Mars from 2012 to now. Each hole was drilled with the drill on its 2.1 meter arm to collect rock powder for analysis. Different colors show different rock types. Gray color inside the hole is the true rock color, meaning it was formed in water. Red color outside is Mars dust. From these holes, Curiosity discovered the ancient lake and organic molecules.

Basic Info.

Name: Curiosity
Mission: Mars Science Laboratory - MSL
Agency: NASA
Location: Mars - Gale Crater
Type: Rover
Size: Length 3 meters, Width 2.7 meters, Height 2.2 meters, Weight 899 kg
Power: Nuclear - RTG, Plutonium, not solar
Instruments: 10 science instruments + 17 cameras
Status: Still operational in 2026
Cost: 2.5 billion dollars
Name: Chosen by Clara Ma, a sixth-grader from Kansas, in an essay contest

26 November 2011

Launched from Cape Canaveral, Florida, on Atlas V rocket, at 15:02:00 UTC

6 August 2012

Landed on Mars at 05:17:57 UTC, in Aeolis Palus inside Gale Crater, site named Bradbury Landing, after Ray Bradbury

December 2012

2-year mission extended indefinitely

2013

Reached Yellowknife Bay, drilled rocks, discovered ancient lake

2014

Reached base of Mount Sharp

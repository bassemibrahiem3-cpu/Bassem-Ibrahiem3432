PERSEVERANCE

Perseverance was built to answer the biggest question in history: Was there life on Mars in the past?

NASA chose Jezero Crater because scientists believed it was a lake 3.5 billion years ago. If there was water, there could have been life.

Perseverance landed in the most dangerous way NASA ever tried, called Sky Crane. The rover was hanging from cables, then the rocket flew away and crashed.

With Perseverance came a small helicopter called Ingenuity. It was the first aircraft to fly on another planet. It was supposed to fly only 5 times, but it flew 72 times.

Perseverance has 7 scientific instruments. The most important job is collecting rock samples. It drills rocks, puts them in tubes, and leaves them on Mars. Future missions will bring these samples back to Earth.

It also carried MOXIE, a small machine that makes oxygen from Mars air. This was the first time oxygen was made on another planet.

Perseverance found organic molecules, which are the building blocks of life. It did not find life, but it found proof that Mars was good for life in the past.

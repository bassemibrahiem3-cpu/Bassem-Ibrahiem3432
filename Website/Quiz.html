Space Rovers Quiz 🚀

--- APOLLO LUNAR ROVER ---

Q1: How was the Apollo Lunar Rover controlled?

A) Normal steering wheel
B) Joystick in the middle
C) Remote from Earth

Q2: What is the fastest speed ever driven on the Moon?

A) 18 km/h (11.2 mph)
B) 100 km/h
C) 5 km/h

Answer: A - 18 km/h, by Eugene Cernan on Apollo 17 in 1972

--- SPIRIT ---

Q1: Where did Spirit land on Mars?

A) Gusev Crater
B) Gale Crater
C) Jezero Crater

Answer: A - Gusev Crater, thought to be an ancient lake

Q2: Why did Spirit die?

A) Got stuck in sand
B) Dust storm
C) Out of gas

Answer: A - Got stuck in soft sand called Troy in 2009, solar panels could not get sun

--- OPPORTUNITY ---

Q1: Opportunity was supposed to work 90 days, how long did it actually work?

A) 90 days
B) 2 years
C) 14 years and 46 days

Answer: C - 14 years and 46 days, from 2004 to 2018

Q2: What was Opportunity's last message?

A) My battery is low and it's getting dark
B) Goodbye
C) Help me

Answer: A - My battery is low and it's getting dark, due to global dust storm

--- CURIOSITY ---

Q1: What did Curiosity find at Mary Anning?

A) Organic molecules Decane and Undecane
B) Gold
C) Humans

Answer: A - Organic molecules Decane, Undecane and Dodecane, evidence for ancient life potential

Q2: What powers Curiosity?

A) Solar panels
B) Nuclear battery RTG
C) Gasoline

Answer: B - Nuclear battery RTG, that's why it still works since 2012

--- PERSEVERANCE ---

Q1: Where did Perseverance land on Mars?

A) Jezero Crater
B) Eagle Crater
C) Gale Crater

Answer: A - Jezero Crater, an ancient river delta

Q2: What did Perseverance bring for the first time in history?

A) Helicopter Ingenuity
B) A human
C) A cat

Answer: A - Helicopter Ingenuity, first thing to fly on another planet besides Earth

Opportunity Rover

The Opportunity Rover Mission

It was an Opportunity to uncover the mysteries of Mars to search if life on Mars has ever existed, even at early stages. The Opportunity Rover gave us more than 217,000 pictures.

But it couldn't prove that life ever existed on Mars; it only collected clues suggesting that Mars once had liquid water and other conditions that might have been suitable for life to exist.

Also, it was originally designed to last for 90 Martian days (92.5 Earth days), but it exceeded it all by 14 years, 47 days. Which was a scientific marvel!

So, let's dive deep into its journey and break it all down!! 🚀

Driving Distances on Mars and the Moon
This chart shows how far each rover has driven, as of October 12, 2026. The orange line is Mars. The gray line is the Moon.

  1. Perseverance: 45.2 km, still driving on Mars
  2. Opportunity: 45.16 km, 2004-2018, on Mars
  3. Lunokhod 2 (USSR): 39 km, 1973, on the Moon
  4. Curiosity: 38.15 km, still driving on Mars
  5. Apollo 17 Lunar Rover: 35.74 km, 1972, on the Moon
  6. Apollo 15 Lunar Rover: 27.8 km, 1971, on the Moon
  7. Apollo 16 Lunar Rover: 27.1 km, 1972, on the Moon
  8. Lunokhod 1 (USSR): 10.5 km, 1970-1971, on the Moon
  9. Spirit: 7.7 km, 2004-2010, on Mars
  10. Sojourner: 0.1 km, 1997, on Mars
The dashed line is the Marathon distance: 42.195 km. Only Perseverance and Opportunity have driven farther than a marathon on another planet.
